Tapir's diel activity patterns

This application presents the daily activity patterns of tapirs in their natural habitats across three distinct Brazilian biomes.

Over the past 25 years, data has been gathered using telemetry collars to track their movements.

Here, you can explore this information by clicking on the designated tapir toes icons, which allows to select a specific biome and tapir individual.

The application then visualizes the average distance traveled by the selected tapir for each hour throughout the day, displayed in a polar plot format that resembles a watch.
